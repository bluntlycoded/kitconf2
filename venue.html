The venue

KIT Auditorium Complex

The Kalam Institute of Technology Auditorium is one of Odisha's premier conference facilities, offering a blend of modern architecture and up-to-date technology. Located in the heart of the KIT campus, the venue provides an inspiring environment for academic and professional gatherings.

Detailed Directions

From Berhampur Railway Station

Take NH-16 towards Bhubaneswar for 12 km. Turn right at the KIT signboard. The auditorium is in the main campus building.

From Bhubaneswar Airport

Take NH-16 southbound for approximately 180 km. Exit at Berhampur and follow signs to the KIT campus. Journey time: ~3.5 hours.

Local Transportation

Auto-rickshaws and buses are available from Berhampur city centre. Taxi services can be arranged through the conference team.
