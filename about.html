Conference Overview

NGVCAI 2026 represents a pivotal gathering of minds committed to the transformation of venture capital. As artificial intelligence reshapes how capital is deployed, this conference serves as a catalyst for innovative solutions that bridge technology, business, and responsible innovation.

Our two-day intensive program brings together leading researchers, industry pioneers, policy makers, and entrepreneurs to explore cutting-edge technologies and methodologies that are reshaping how startups are funded, built, and scaled in the 21st century.

Six tracks

Conference Themes

Six tracks covering where artificial intelligence meets venture capital, entrepreneurship, and technology investment.

AI-Driven Deal Sourcing & Due Diligence

Machine learning models that surface, screen, and score early-stage opportunities faster and more consistently than manual pipelines.

Generative AI in Startup Ecosystems

How foundation models are reshaping product development, go-to-market, and the cost structure of building a company.

Predictive Analytics for Portfolio Management

Forecasting portfolio performance, follow-on timing, and exit windows using data-driven signals rather than intuition alone.

Fintech & Algorithmic Investment Models

Automated underwriting, alternative data, revenue-based financing, and the infrastructure behind programmatic capital allocation.

AI Governance, Ethics & Investment Risk

Bias, explainability, regulation, and the fiduciary questions raised when algorithms influence who receives funding.

Emerging Markets & Digital Entrepreneurship

AI-enabled venture building outside established hubs, and what it takes to fund innovation in developing economies.

Our story

How NGVCAI Began

NGVCAI was conceived from a vision to create a premier platform where capital meets technology. As artificial intelligence reshapes every stage of the venture cycle, the need for innovative, technology-driven solutions becomes increasingly critical.

Hosted by Kalam Institute of Technology, Odisha, India, the conference represents the culmination of extensive planning and collaboration with leading investors, researchers, and technology leaders worldwide.

Our goal is to establish this conference as an annual gathering that not only showcases cutting-edge research but also facilitates meaningful partnerships and drives real-world implementation of AI-enabled investment practice.
