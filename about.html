Conference Overview

The NGVCAI 2026 represents a pivotal gathering of minds committed to the transformation of venture capital. As artificial intelligence reshapes how capital is deployed, this conference serves as a catalyst for innovative solutions that bridge technology, business, and responsible innovation.

Our two-day intensive program brings together leading researchers, industry pioneers, policy makers, and entrepreneurs to explore cutting-edge technologies and methodologies that are reshaping how startups are funded, built, and scaled in the 21st century.

Our Mission

To accelerate responsible adoption of artificial intelligence across the investment lifecycle through research-driven collaboration between academia, industry, and the investor community.

Conference Themes

Six tracks covering where artificial intelligence meets venture capital, entrepreneurship, and technology investment.

AI-Driven Deal Sourcing & Due Diligence

Machine learning models that surface, screen, and score early-stage opportunities faster and more consistently than manual pipelines.

Generative AI in Startup Ecosystems

How foundation models are reshaping product development, go-to-market, and the cost structure of building a company.

Predictive Analytics for Portfolio Management

Forecasting portfolio performance, follow-on timing, and exit windows using data-driven signals rather than intuition alone.

Fintech & Algorithmic Investment Models

Automated underwriting, alternative data, revenue-based financing, and the infrastructure behind programmatic capital allocation.

AI Governance, Ethics & Investment Risk

Bias, explainability, regulation, and the fiduciary questions raised when algorithms influence who receives funding.

Emerging Markets & Digital Entrepreneurship

AI-enabled venture building outside established hubs, and what it takes to fund innovation in developing economies.

Our Story

The NGVCAI 2026 was conceived from a vision to create a premier platform where capital meets technology. As artificial intelligence reshapes every stage of the venture cycle, the need for innovative, technology-driven solutions becomes increasingly critical.

Hosted by the prestigious Kalam Institute of Technology, Odisha, India, this inaugural conference represents the culmination of extensive planning and collaboration with leading investors, researchers, and technology leaders worldwide.

Our goal is to establish this conference as an annual gathering that not only showcases cutting-edge research but also facilitates meaningful partnerships and drives real-world implementation of AI-enabled investment practice.

Expected Outcomes

The NGVCAI 2026 aims to deliver tangible outcomes that advance AI-enabled investment practice globally.

Innovation Catalyst

Spark breakthrough innovations through cross-sector collaboration and knowledge exchange between researchers and industry professionals.

Strategic Partnerships

Facilitate meaningful partnerships between academia, industry, and government to accelerate responsible AI adoption in venture capital.

Market Implementation

Bridge the gap between research and market application, turning innovative ideas into commercially viable ventures.
