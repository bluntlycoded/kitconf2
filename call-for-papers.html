Scope

Topics of Interest

Submissions are welcome across all six conference tracks, and on any adjacent topic where AI meets capital.

AI-Driven Deal Sourcing & Due Diligence

Machine learning models that surface, screen, and score early-stage opportunities.

Generative AI in Startup Ecosystems

Foundation models reshaping product development, go-to-market, and cost structure.

Predictive Analytics for Portfolio Management

Forecasting performance, follow-on timing, and exit windows from data-driven signals.

Fintech & Algorithmic Investment Models

Automated underwriting, alternative data, and programmatic capital allocation.

AI Governance, Ethics & Investment Risk

Bias, explainability, regulation, and fiduciary duty when algorithms allocate funding.

Emerging Markets & Digital Entrepreneurship

AI-enabled venture building beyond established hubs and in developing economies.

Publication

Published in Partnership with Wiley

All accepted and presented papers will appear in the NGVCAI 2026 conference proceedings, published in partnership with Wiley and submitted for Scopus indexing.
